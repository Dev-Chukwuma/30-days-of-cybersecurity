# Day 29 - Cybersecurity Portfolio

## Topics Covered
- Turning 30 days of hands-on work into a curated portfolio piece
- Selecting standout projects and framing them as case studies (what I did / what I found / what it demonstrates)
- Structuring proof-of-work for recruiters and hiring managers

## What I Learned
A raw folder of daily work doesn't tell a story on its own — a portfolio's job is to answer 
"what can this person actually do?" in under 60 seconds. The difference between a repo and 
a portfolio piece is narrative: not just "I did X," but the problem, the action, the finding, 
and the skill it proves.

## Hands-On
- Selected 7 standout pieces spanning the full skillset: OS/networking foundations (Days 1-10), 
  Python tooling (Day 13), offensive security (Day 15), packet investigation (Day 20-21), 
  SIEM work (Day 24), incident response (Day 25), and MITRE ATT&CK + SOC investigation (Day 27-28)
- Wrote a PORTFOLIO.md with each piece framed as: What I did → What I found/built → What it demonstrates
- Added a closing summary tying all the pieces into one overall skillset statement

## Key Insight
Quality over completeness — a portfolio isn't a table of contents, it's a curated argument 
for why you're hireable.
